### 5. Learnings

- **`git log` is not a complete picture of what is about to enter the repo.**
  The 48 MB in `src/music/` is invisible to every command in this skill's
  "gather the shared facts" block except `git status --short`, and even there it
  is one unremarkable line — the size only appears if you ask. **Behavioural
  change:** when `git status` shows an untracked *directory*, I will `du -sh` it
  and check `git check-ignore` before reporting the day as quiet. Proposed rule
  for the `daily-report` skill: add `du -sh` on untracked directories to step 2's
  shared-facts gather.

- **An unset `goals.md` is more informative than a filled-in one would have
  been.** Writing section 1 forced the question "what would a goal here even be
  measured from", and the honest answer for the most important candidate —
  backups — is that it has no measurement because it has no existence. A goal
  that cannot be measured from a command is a goal that will quietly stop being
  reported. **Behavioural change:** every goal I propose names the command, not
  the intention.

- **The composite cap is a sequencing instruction, not a grade.** `min(D,R,S)+2`
  means the next three things I might route to `backend` or `frontend` cannot
  raise the architecture score at all, and that is *fine* — most good feature
  work scores zero everywhere. But it does mean that if someone asks "what should
  we do next to improve the architecture", the answer is not a refactor. It is
  candidate A, and it is roughly an afternoon.

- **Proposed goal change:** adopt candidates A, B and C above, in that order, or
  tell me which to drop. I will not write them to `goals.md` myself.
